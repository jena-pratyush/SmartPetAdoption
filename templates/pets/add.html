Legal Notice — Read Before Listing

Not all animals can be legally adopted or rehomed in India. Listing a protected or wild animal is a criminal offence under Indian law and may result in imprisonment of up to 7 years and heavy fines.

You must NOT list animals that are:

  • Listed under Schedules I–IV of the Wildlife Protection Act, 1972 (e.g. tigers, leopards, peacocks, parrots, tortoises, many snakes & lizards)
  • Protected under CITES (Convention on International Trade in Endangered Species)
  • Wild-caught animals — whether injured, abandoned, or hand-raised
  • Exotic animals acquired without a valid permit or NOC from the CWLW

Generally legal to rehome (domestic pets):

  • Dogs & Cats
  • Rabbits, guinea pigs, hamsters
  • Common domestic fish & birds (budgerigars, cockatiels — NOT Indian parrots or hill mynas)
Official Resources: Wildlife Protection Act, 1972 (Full Text) CITES — MoEFCC India PAWS India — Illegal Wildlife Trade Guide
